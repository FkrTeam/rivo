01Studio

We design buildings that hold still while the light moves through them.

Founded by a small group of architects, the studio works at every scale from a garden wall to a civic hall. Each project begins with a physical model: weight, proportion and the fall of daylight are decided before a single drawing is made.

03Approach

Three things we return to.

IWeight
Buildings should look like they were always going to be there. We work with mass first and openings second.
IILight
Daylight is the material we cannot buy. Every room is planned around where it enters and where it rests.
IIIMeasure
A few dimensions, repeated with care, do more than many. The plan sets the rhythm; the section keeps it.
