01 / About

A modern architectural millwork partner.

RIVO coordinates design, detailing, fabrication and field execution so each architectural element reads as one intentional system.

Purpose
To shape interiors through millwork that is clear, coordinated and built with disciplined craft.
Promise
Design intelligence, manufacturing control and installation-ready detailing in one dependable system.
Positioning
A modern architectural millwork partner for hospitality, residential, retail and high-performance commercial projects.

RIVO

Rhythm in Volume

RIVO is a constructed name that expresses how millwork organizes space: repeated modules, measured reveals, material transitions and sculpted architectural volume.

02 / Capabilities

Four stages. One system.

  1. 1

    Design

    Intent, proportion and material logic resolved with the design team before anything is cut.

  2. 2

    Detailing

    Shop drawings, sections and joint details. Dimensions, finishes and revisions kept on record.

  3. 3

    Fabrication

    Modules built to the approved drawing set, finished and checked against it in the shop.

  4. 4

    Field execution

    Sequenced delivery and installation, coordinated with the site so each element lands as drawn.
